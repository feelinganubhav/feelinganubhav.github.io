Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Meta app named feeling_Anubhav, a small automation tool built and run by Anubhav Ranjan for his own Instagram accounts, @feeling_anubhav, @anubhavbuildsai and @kuchu.puchu.diaries. It is not a service anyone signs up for, and it has no user accounts. It is also the privacy policy for this website, which collects nothing about you beyond what your browser sends to any web server.

What Instagram data the app uses

  • Comments on my own posts. The app reads comments on posts from my own accounts, including the commenter's username and the comment ID, to spot keyword comments (for example "jarvis").
  • Replies and DMs to people who comment. When someone comments a keyword, the app sends one reply and a direct message with the free material they asked for, and reads that single conversation only to see whether they answered (for example "DONE") or shared a reel to the account.
  • Insights for my own accounts. The app reads reach, views, likes, shares and similar numbers for my own posts and accounts, to learn what works.
  • Whether a person follows my account, only when they have messaged the account.

Why

Only to deliver what the person asked for in their comment, and to understand how my own content performs. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone.

What we do not do

  • We do not read DMs from people who did not comment a keyword or message the account.
  • We do not collect passwords, phone numbers or payment details.
  • We do not send your data to third parties.

How long we keep it

A small activity log (username, comment ID, what was sent, time) is kept on the owner's own computer. Message text from other people is not stored beyond that log. Entries are deleted on request and cleared after 12 months at the latest.

Your choices and deletion

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you. See the data deletion instructions. You can also remove the app at any time from Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites.
