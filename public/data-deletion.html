How to delete your data

The Meta app feeling_Anubhav keeps only a small log of who commented a keyword on my Instagram posts and what was sent back. We do not sell or share this data. To have your entries removed:

  1. Send a DM to @feeling_anubhav on Instagram saying "delete my data", or email anubhavr204@gmail.com from any address with your Instagram username in the message.
  2. We find every log entry for your username or ID and delete it.
  3. We confirm by reply within 7 days.

If you remove the app from your Instagram settings (Settings, Apps and websites), it can no longer see anything about your account. Deletion requests cost nothing.
